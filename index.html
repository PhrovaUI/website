01 ABOUT THE PROJECT

A project still finding its melody.

PhrovaUI is a private Android custom ROM project built upon the crDroid ecosystem and inspired by Phrolova from Wuthering Waves. Developed as a personal endeavor rather than a public-facing project, its goal is to maintain a smooth, distinctive, and reliable software experience. It openly credits its crDroid codebase and upstream sources while providing a unique, personalized spin on the Android open-source ecosystem.

Development is ongoing.
