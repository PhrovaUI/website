01 ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built in silence. Made to hit.

PhrovaUI is a Android custom ROM project built upon the crDroid ecosystem and inspired by Phrolova from Wuthering Waves. Developed as a personal endeavor rather than a public-facing project, its goal is to maintain a smooth, distinctive, and reliable software experience. It openly credits its crDroid codebase and upstream sources while providing a unique, personalized spin on the Android open-source ecosystem.

Development is ongoing.

No. It is a personal endeavour, not a public-facing project. Follow along if you want to watch it take shape.
